Cleaning science

Why chemistry matters as much as pressure.

Exterior staining is often biological, mineral, or pollution-based. High pressure alone can damage surfaces or push water where it should not go. Our process uses controlled chemistry, dwell time, agitation when needed, and responsible rinsing to support a better clean.

pH

Solution selection

Cleaning products are chosen for the surface and contaminant type, not applied as a one-size-fits-all treatment.

H₂O

Water control

Crews plan rinse direction, runoff awareness, and surrounding area protection before application begins.

O₂

Organic release

Algae, mold, mildew, and biofilm are treated with dwell time so buildup releases with less mechanical force.
